Meal Prep on a Budget: The Beginner's System That Works

Two hours on Sunday. Five days of effortless meals. No complicated recipes, no special equipment — just a simple, repeatable system for real kitchens with real budgets.

📖 7 min read Beginner Guide
The System

How Budget Meal Prep Works

🛒

1. Plan & Shop

Use the free Grocery Budget Planner to set your weekly budget. Then plan 2–3 proteins, 1 grain, and vegetables before shopping.

⏱️

2. Cook in Batches

Cook 2 proteins, 1 large grain pot, and a sheet pan of vegetables simultaneously on Sunday. Everything finishes in about 2 hours.

🥡

3. Mix & Match

Combine the same building blocks into different meals each day using simple sauces and seasonings. Saves money, reduces waste, and removes decision fatigue.
