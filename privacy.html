Privacy Policy

We keep things simple. Your data stays with you.

Last updated: January 2024

Overview

MyFitWealthJourney.com is a static informational website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This policy explains what information we collect (if any), how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our free tools (Grocery Budget Planner, Meal Cost Calculator, Protein Cost Calculator) operate entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit the data you enter into these calculators. Your inputs remain on your device only.

We do not have user accounts, and we do not require you to create one to use any feature of this site.

Information We May Collect

Analytics

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics service (such as Google Analytics or Plausible) to understand general traffic patterns. This may include pages visited, general geographic location (country/region level), device type, and referral source. This data is aggregated and not linked to individual users.

Contact Form

If you contact us through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content for the sole purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not share this information with third parties or add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies required for analytics. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Third-Party Services

This website may be hosted on a third-party platform (such as Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages) which may collect server logs including IP addresses for security and operational purposes. These services have their own privacy policies.

We may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Display Advertising

This site may display ads through Google AdSense or similar services. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights

Since we collect minimal personal data, there is typically nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you submitted a contact form and wish to have that data removed, please email us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
