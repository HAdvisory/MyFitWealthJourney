This Week's Smart Habit

One Small Change. Real Impact.

Cook once. Eat twice.

Pick any meal you're already planning to cook this week. Make a double batch.

Two servings of rice becomes four. One pound of ground turkey becomes two dinners. A pot of beans made on Monday is lunch on Wednesday. You spend exactly the same time cooking. You eliminate one decision, one last-minute purchase, and likely one takeout order later in the week.

For most households, a single eliminated takeout order saves $12–$18. Do this three times in a month and you've freed up $36–$54 without changing what you eat or learning a new recipe.

This is meal prep reduced to its smallest useful form. No Sunday system required. No special containers. Just: whatever you're already making — make more of it.
