About This Tool

The Grocery Budget Planner takes your weekly budget and household size, then allocates spending across grocery categories — produce, protein, dairy, pantry staples, and extras — using evidence-based budget ratios. Adjust your diet preference and meals-at-home count to shift the allocation to match how your household actually eats.

Who it's for: Anyone trying to plan a grocery run with intention, from solo shoppers on $50/week to families of four working with $200. It's especially useful if you keep overspending in one category without realizing where the money goes.

How to read the results: The category breakdown is a starting point, not a rule. The "per meal" figure is the most actionable number — if it's higher than you expected, consider batch cooking or substituting cheaper proteins that week. Green is good; adjust until the plan feels realistic for your next trip.
